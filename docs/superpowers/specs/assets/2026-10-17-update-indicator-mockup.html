How it behaves
  • × dismisses this update: the card closes and the indicator disappears until the next launch or a newer version. "Check for updates…" brings it back any time.
  • Clicking outside the card or pressing Escape just closes the card; the indicator stays.
  • Buttons never wrap or squash: the card is wider and the labels stay on one line.
